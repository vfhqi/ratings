Market: the Stoxx Europe 600 and each industry, rebased to the indexhover for any day's levels

Each industry is the equal-weighted average of its members' daily price moves (members need a price for the whole 12 months), started at the Stoxx Europe 600's level on the first day, so a line above the index has beaten it over the period. The figure after each name is its move over the period.

The book, the holdings needing action first sorted: first the holdings the rule sells at the next Tuesday session; then a tranche in its release window; then the Middle Innings Core Investments by the number of Sell Criteria met; then the rest; within each, the nearest to selling first

How to read this page: each row is one holding, and the holdings the rule says to act on come first. Read across a row to see its status, what the order sheet says, which of the six Sell Criteria are met, how near it is to selling, its size, what we could lose, whether the rule allows an add, and whether it has moved nearer to selling since last week. Click a row to open the holding's own page.
The six boxes, left to right: Industry RS below 30; the 200-day average of relative price falling; Sector RS below 50 with Stock RS below 70; Stock RS below 50 with analysts cutting; relative price more than 25% below its 52-week high; a tranche 30% below its highest close since purchase. Red: met. Amber: one part of a two-part criterion met. Grey outline: information only (a Probing Bet, whose moving-average stop governs). Since last week compares the nearest tranche's distance to its 30% level with the readings a week earlier: green when it moved further from that level, red when it moved nearer, grey when it moved less than 2 points. No profit and loss is shown on this page.
