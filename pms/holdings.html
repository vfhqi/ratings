PMS Per-stock Dashboardall holdings, in risk order; each row opens its holding's page

The book in risk order sorted: a tranche in its release window first, then the Middle Innings Core Investments by the number of Sell Criteria met, then the Early Innings Probing Bets; within each, the nearest to selling first

How to read this page: each row is one holding, riskiest first. Read across a row to see its status, what the order sheet says, which of the six Sell Criteria are met, how near it is to selling, its size, what we could lose, whether the rule allows an add, and whether it has moved nearer to selling since last week. Click a row to open the holding's own page.
The six boxes, left to right: Industry RS below 30; the 200-day average of relative price falling; Sector RS below 50 with Stock RS below 70; Stock RS below 50 with analysts cutting; relative price more than 25% below its 52-week high; a tranche 30% below its highest close since purchase. Red: met. Amber: one part of a two-part criterion met. Grey outline: information only (a Probing Bet, whose moving-average stop governs). Room to add: whether the rule allows another tranche to be bought now, and if not, why not. Since last week: whether the holding's nearest tranche has moved further from its 30% level (green, further from selling) or nearer to it (red, nearer to selling) since the readings a week earlier; a change of less than 2 points reads as about the same. No profit and loss is shown on this page.
