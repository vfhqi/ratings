How to use: 20 investment slots × 6 tranche rows (PB1, PB2, Core MM 1–4). Type %AUM as 3 for 3%. Pick a date + stop MA. MA→stop buffer defaults to 2% (= stop is MA × 0.98) and is editable per tranche. Derived columns auto-fill. Drag the ⋮⋮ handle to move tranches between slots — drop a PB onto an MM to promote. Cross-investment drag blocked.
Slot caps: PB1/2 = 2.5%, MM1 = 4%, MM2 = 3%, MM3 = 2%, MM4 = 1%. PB stops: 5D/10D/20D/50D. MM stops: any of 5 MAs. One stop rule (11-Sep-2026): a stop is hit on the first close at or below it and stays triggered while the price keeps closing there; the exit is confirmed on the third consecutive close at or below (sell the next trading day). A close back above on day 1 or 2 resets the count. No weekly close. Once a slice has closed 20% up, its entry price is a floor and the higher level binds.
Row colours by MA for stop-loss: orange=20D, blue=50D, teal=100D, magenta=150D, slate=200D. Breach states override. Empty slots can have a ticker typed in. New MM tranches warn if no PB exists for that investment (override permitted for backfill). Connect to disk for auto-save (auto-reconnects on reload).
